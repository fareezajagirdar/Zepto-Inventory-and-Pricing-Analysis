Query 9 — Counting Out-of-Stock Products by Category

Business question:
Which categories have the highest number of out-of-stock products?

How we approached the problem:
We filtered the records to include only out-of-stock products. We grouped these records by category, counted the products in each group, and sorted the counts in descending order.

SQL concepts used:
- SELECT — selected category and out-of-stock count.
- WHERE — filtered out-of-stock records.
- COUNT() — counted records.
- GROUP BY — grouped records by category.
- ORDER BY — sorted the counts.
- DESC — arranged results from highest to lowest.

Query:sql
SELECT Category, COUNT(*) AS out_of_stock_count
FROM zepto_analysis.zepto_v2
WHERE outOfStock = 'TRUE'
GROUP BY Category
ORDER BY out_of_stock_count DESC;

What the query solved:
Compared out-of-stock record counts across product categories.

What we found:
Cooking Essentials and Munchies had the highest out-of-stock counts with 64 records each. Fruits & Vegetables had the lowest count with 6 records.
