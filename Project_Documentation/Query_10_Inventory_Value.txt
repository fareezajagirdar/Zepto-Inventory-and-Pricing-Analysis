Query 10 — Estimating Inventory Value by Category

Business question:
What is the estimated value of available inventory in each category?

How we approached the problem:
We multiplied the discounted selling price of each product by its available quantity to estimate the inventory value. We summed these values category-wise, converted prices from paise to rupees by dividing by 100, rounded the result to two decimal places, and sorted categories by estimated inventory value.

SQL concepts used:
- SELECT — selected categories and calculated inventory value.
- SUM() — calculated total inventory value.
- ROUND() — rounded values.
- GROUP BY — grouped inventory value by category.
- ORDER BY — sorted the calculated values.

Query:sql
SELECT Category,
ROUND(SUM(discountedSellingPrice * availableQuantity) / 100, 2) AS inventory_value
FROM zepto_analysis.zepto_v2
GROUP BY Category
ORDER BY inventory_value DESC;

What the query solved:
Estimated the value of available inventory based on discounted selling prices.

What we found:
Cooking Essentials and Munchies had the highest estimated inventory value at ₹337,131 each. Fruits & Vegetables had the lowest at ₹10,846.
