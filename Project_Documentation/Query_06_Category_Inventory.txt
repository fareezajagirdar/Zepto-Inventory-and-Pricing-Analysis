Query 6 — Analyzing Inventory Quantity by Category

Business question:
Which categories have the highest total available inventory?

How we approached the problem:
We grouped products by category and summed their available quantities. We sorted the totals in descending order to identify categories with the largest recorded inventory.

SQL concepts used:
- SELECT — selected category and inventory quantity.
- SUM() — calculated total available quantity.
- GROUP BY — grouped inventory by category.
- ORDER BY — sorted inventory totals.
- DESC — arranged totals from highest to lowest.

Query:sql
SELECT Category, SUM(availableQuantity) AS total_inventory
FROM zepto_analysis.zepto_v2
GROUP BY Category
ORDER BY total_inventory DESC;

What the query solved:
Compared the total recorded inventory quantities across categories.

What we found:
Cooking Essentials and Munchies had the highest total recorded inventory quantity of 2,179 each.
