Query 8 — Identifying Out-of-Stock Products

Business question:
Which products are currently marked as out of stock?

How we approached the problem:
We selected the product name, category, available quantity, and stock status. We filtered the dataset to include only products whose outOfStock status was TRUE and displayed a sample of 10 records.

SQL concepts used:
- SELECT — selected product and stock details.
- WHERE — filtered out-of-stock records.
- LIMIT — displayed a sample of 10 records.

Query:sql
SELECT name, Category, availableQuantity, outOfStock
FROM zepto_analysis.zepto_v2
WHERE outOfStock = 'TRUE'
LIMIT 10;

What the query solved:
Retrieved a sample of products that were unavailable for purchase.

What we found:
Out-of-stock products appeared across multiple categories, including Fruits & Vegetables and Cooking Essentials, with available quantity recorded as 0.
