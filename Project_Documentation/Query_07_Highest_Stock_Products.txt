Query 7 — Finding Products with the Highest Available Stock

Business question:
Which products have the highest available stock quantity?

How we approached the problem:
We selected product names, categories, and available quantities. We sorted the available quantities in descending order and displayed the top 10 records.

SQL concepts used:
- SELECT — selected product details.
- ORDER BY — sorted stock quantities.
- DESC — arranged quantities from highest to lowest.
- LIMIT — displayed the top 10 records.

Query:sql
SELECT name, Category, availableQuantity
FROM zepto_analysis.zepto_v2
ORDER BY availableQuantity DESC
LIMIT 10;

What the query solved:
Identified products with the highest available quantities.

What we found:
The maximum available quantity in the dataset was 6. Several products shared this quantity, with Home & Cleaning appearing frequently in the top results.
