Query 12 — Finding the Heaviest Products

Business question:
Which products have the highest weight in the dataset?

How we approached the problem:
We selected the product name, category, and weight in grams. We sorted the product weights in descending order and displayed the top 10 records.

SQL concepts used:
- SELECT — selected product details.
- ORDER BY — sorted products by weight.
- DESC — arranged weights from highest to lowest.
- LIMIT — displayed the top 10 records.

Query:sql
SELECT name, Category, weightInGms
FROM zepto_analysis.zepto_v2
ORDER BY weightInGms DESC
LIMIT 10;

What the query solved:
Identified products with the highest recorded weight.

What we found:
Aashirvaad Atta and Pillsbury Chakki Fresh Atta were among the heaviest products at 10,000 grams (10 kg).
